Market, Consumer & GTM Analysis Case Study

Overview

Supporting Material: View Strategy Deck (PPT) This case study analyzes Maggi’s brand resilience, market positioning, and growth opportunities in the Indian FMCG space. The focus is on identifying strategic enhancements to align with evolving health-conscious consumer trends.

Context & Background

Maggi is one of India’s most trusted FMCG brands, known for convenience and affordability. However, increasing health awareness, regulatory scrutiny, and changing consumer preferences pose challenges to sustained growth.

Problem Statement

How can Maggi continue to drive growth while addressing health concerns and maintaining trust among Gen Z and health-conscious consumers?

Market & Consumer Analysis

  • Conducted TAM–SAM–SOM analysis to evaluate market potential
  • Analyzed consumer behavior shifts toward healthier food alternatives
  • Benchmarked competitors offering protein-rich and millet-based products

Proposed Strategy

  • Healthy Maggi Line: Protein-rich, millet-based, low-sodium variants
  • Brand Positioning: Transition from comfort food to balanced lifestyle food
  • Regional Customization: Local flavors to drive regional adoption

Go-To-Market Strategy

  • Influencer-led campaigns with fitness and wellness creators
  • Partnerships with gyms and fitness platforms
  • Digital-first launches targeting Gen Z audiences

Projected Impact

  • 10–15% increase in urban and Gen Z user base
  • $100–200M incremental revenue opportunity
  • Improved NPS and brand trust perception

Key Learnings

This case study improved my understanding of brand-led product strategy, market sizing, GTM planning, and aligning product positioning with evolving consumer expectations.
