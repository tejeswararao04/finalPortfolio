Overview

Comparing standard CNNs and efficient architectures

Jan 2026 - Feb 2026 | PyTorch, CNNs, ResNet, Grad-CAM

Image classification study on Oxford Flowers-102 comparing a standard CNN with a depthwise-separable CNN for accuracy vs efficiency trade-offs.

Key Results

  • Standard CNN: 29.9% accuracy on Oxford Flowers-102
  • Depthwise-separable CNN: 25.6% accuracy with far fewer parameters
  • Parameter reduction from 415K to 89K (4.6x)
  • Applied data augmentation, Grad-CAM, and confusion-matrix analysis to evaluate generalization and class-level misclassification

Tech Stack and Methods

  • PyTorch, CNNs, ResNet-style blocks
  • Depthwise-separable convolutions for efficiency
  • Data augmentation for generalization
  • Grad-CAM for model interpretability
  • Confusion-matrix analysis for class-level errors
