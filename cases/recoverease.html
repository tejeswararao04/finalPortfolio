Overview

Supporting Material: View Presentation (PPT) RecoverEASE is a digital physiotherapy product concept designed to reduce rehabilitation dropout rates by combining AI-assisted exercise guidance with human physiotherapist support. The case study focuses on product strategy, problem validation, and go-to-market planning.

Problem

A large percentage of patients discontinue physiotherapy after initial sessions due to high costs, limited access to clinics, lack of motivation, and poor adherence to prescribed exercises. This leads to incomplete recovery and long-term health issues.

Target Users

  • Patients recovering from orthopedic injuries
  • Post-surgery rehabilitation patients
  • Users in urban and semi-urban areas with limited access to physiotherapy clinics

Proposed Solution

RecoverEASE offers a hybrid physiotherapy experience combining AI-based motion tracking and periodic human physiotherapist interventions to improve adherence and recovery outcomes.

  • Mirror Mode: Real-time AI feedback on exercise posture
  • Recovery Buddy: Peer support and motivation feature
  • Weekly Physio Sessions: Human oversight for trust and accountability

Product-Market Fit & GTM Strategy

The product targets users who drop out due to affordability and access barriers. A tiered subscription model allows flexibility while B2B partnerships enable scale.

  • B2C subscription plans (basic AI guidance to premium hybrid care)
  • B2B partnerships with clinics, insurers, and corporate wellness programs
  • Referral-led acquisition via physiotherapists

Metrics & Impact

  • Projected LTV:CAC ratio > 3:1
  • Estimated Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM): $50–250M
  • Improved rehab adherence and recovery completion rates

Key Learnings

This case study strengthened my understanding of user behavior in healthcare, PMF validation, GTM strategy, and designing products that balance technology, business sustainability, and human trust.
