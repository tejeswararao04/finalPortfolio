Overview

QWIPO is India's first FMCG supply chain management product that onboarded distributors and is powered by ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce). The platform digitizes distributor-retailer operations - ordering, logistics, beat planning, and fulfilment - for FMCG brands.

As a Product Management Intern (Hybrid, Hyderabad), I worked at the intersection of product, engineering, and business - defining workflows, writing PRDs, doing on-ground discovery, and contributing to core logistics-tech initiatives with measurable business impact.

Role and Scope

  • Role: Product Management Intern
  • Duration: Jun 2026 - Present
  • Location: Hyderabad, India (Hybrid)
  • Stakeholders: Product, Engineering, Sales (70 salespeople), Distributors and Retailers (10000+)
  • Ways of working: Agile, grooming iterations, PRD to user stories to handoff to tech

Key Contributions

1. 0 to 1 Role-Based Logistics Dashboard - Prototype from Scratch

  • Built a 0 to 1 prototype of a role-based logistics dashboard, defining user workflows, product requirements, and access controls
  • Refined through multiple grooming iterations before handoff to the tech team
  • Focus: operational visibility, beat execution, and distributor-centric UX

2. On-Ground Discovery - JTBDs from the Field

  • Conducted 10+ on-ground user interviews with distributors and retailers
  • Uncovered Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBDs) and translated customer pain points into prioritized product improvements
  • Mapped pain points in ordering, delivery, and reconciliation

3. OTA Update Research - From Friction to Strategy

Researched OTA update mechanisms to address UX friction from mandatory hard updates in the existing NativeScript codebase.

  • Quantified impact: 3.5M minutes (58K hours) of sales effort per update cycle across 70 salespeople and 10000+ retailers
  • Researched OTA (Over-The-Air) update mechanisms and trade-offs
  • Informed strategic decision: migration from NativeScript to React Native for seamless OTA updates

4. Vehicle Beat Optimization - India's First Solution

Contributed to building India's first vehicle beat optimization solution for distributor logistics.

  • Optimized delivery beats to consolidate routes and improve vehicle utilization
  • Impact: enabled distributors to eliminate one delivery vehicle and save approximately Rs. 45K per month
  • Direct business outcome tied to cost reduction and operational efficiency

5. PRDs and User Stories - Driving Agile Delivery

  • Authored PRDs and 70+ user stories, collaborating with Product, Engineering, and business stakeholders to prioritize features
  • Drove backlog prioritization and sprint readiness - bridging business needs and engineering feasibility
