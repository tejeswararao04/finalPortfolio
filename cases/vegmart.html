Overview

VegMart - Digital Restaurant to Mandi Procurement

Course: Market Research and Validation | Project: Veg-Mart

We went on ground near Paradise Restaurant, studied how they order daily fruits and vegetables from mandi suppliers and distributors, and validated our problem statement with primary data.

Problem Statement

Daily bulk vegetable purchasing is manual and fragmented

Fine-dining restaurants struggle to efficiently manage daily bulk vegetable purchasing across multiple wholesalers. They rely on time-consuming phone calls, WhatsApp communication and daily mandi visits to compare prices, identify suppliers and make cost-effective purchasing decisions.

Restaurants procure fresh vegetables daily from multiple mandi wholesalers. Finding suppliers, checking prices, placing bulk orders and coordinating deliveries is largely manual. This makes it hard to compare prices, find the best supplier, maintain consistent procurement and track purchasing.

VegMart aim: digitalize restaurant to mandi procurement in one platform where restaurants can discover local wholesalers, compare prices, place bulk orders and manage recurring purchases.

Target Customers and Stakeholders

Who we focused on

Broader market: fine-dining restaurants, casual dining, cafes, dhabas, street food and small eateries, cloud kitchens.

Initial validation scope: fine-dining restaurants, narrowed to understand procurement in depth.

  • Primary: Restaurants (buyers) and Mandi Wholesalers (sellers)
  • Secondary: Mandi Operators and APMC, Farmers, Delivery and Transport Providers

Market Size

How big is the opportunity

  • India produces approx. 220 million tonnes of vegetables annually. Commercial vegetable market estimated around USD 100B, approx. Rs. 9.5 lakh crore
  • India has approx. 5 lakh or more restaurants. Food services market approx. Rs. 5.69 lakh crore
  • Restaurant vegetable and fruit procurement estimated at approx. Rs. 40,000 crore annually - the relevant market for VegMart

Field Research - Paradise and Pista House

On-ground validation near Paradise Restaurant

Scope: Hyderabad to fine-dining to Paradise to Gachibowli branch. Sources: Branch Manager, Cooks at Gachibowli.

  • Visited Paradise Gachibowli with a structured questionnaire. Met Head Chef and cooks. Studied how vegetables are purchased, suppliers selected, prices compared, orders placed, quality checked and rejections handled
  • Team shared procurement info but did not permit taking complete records due to confidentiality. We noted shared info and recurring patterns, including pricing from Sri Sai Traders, a regular supplier
  • Cross-check at Pista House, DLF: compared prices of common vegetables. Prices differed between suppliers and contexts, confirming that the same vegetable can have different quoted prices by supplier - price comparison matters

Studied: how wholesalers are found, how prices are compared, how bulk orders are placed, order frequency, delivery coordination, quality checks, rejection handling, payments, recurring purchases, and decisions when prices vary.

Finding: process depends on existing relationships, phone calls, WhatsApp and physical mandi visits.

Dataset - 112 Purchases, 18 Vegetables, 4 Suppliers, 7 Days

From field notes to structured data

Since complete historical records were not shared, the dataset combines noted pricing and patterns from primary interactions plus synthetic data based on observed patterns (categories, quantities, supplier selection, quotations, purchase prices, quality ratings, rejections, payment modes).

Result: 112 purchase line items covering 18 vegetables, 4 suppliers and 7 days of procurement.

KPI Framework - Key Numbers

1. Average Purchase Price - Rs. 47.15 per kg overall

  • Formula: Total Purchase Amount / Total Quantity Ordered
  • Highest: Garlic Rs. 150.64 per kg, Ginger Rs. 137.35 per kg, Drumstick Rs. 76.13 per kg, Coriander Rs. 74.52 per kg, Capsicum Green Rs. 68.34 per kg

2. Supplier Price Variation - Rs. 8.05 per kg average

  • Formula: Highest Quoted Price - Lowest Quoted Price, averaged across purchases
  • Highest: Coriander Rs. 26.14 per kg, Mint Rs. 13.67 per kg, Ginger Rs. 11.00 per kg, Green Chilli Rs. 10.86 per kg, Drumstick Rs. 10.14 per kg

3. Potential Cost Saving - Rs. 1,571 (2.64% of spend)

  • Formula: Actual Purchase Amount - (Quantity x Lowest Quoted Price), summed. Saving % = Saving / Total Spend x 100
  • Top contributors: Potato Rs. 262.10, Tomato Rs. 261.10, Capsicum Green Rs. 151.50, Drumstick Rs. 115.60, Brinjal Rs. 111.60

4. Supplier Dependency - Sri Sai Traders 54.49%

  • Formula: Supplier Spend / Total Spend x 100
  • Sri Sai Traders 54.49% (60 line items), Gudimalkapur Fresh Supply 22.27%, Venkateswara Vegetables 11.78%, Monda Market Agro Agencies 11.47%

5. Quality Rejection - 0.89% overall (11.2 kg of approx. 1262 kg)

  • Formula: Rejected Qty / Ordered Qty x 100
  • Highest: Carrot 6.09%, Ladyfinger Okra 3.96%, Mint 3.94%, Cabbage 3.24%, Brinjal 2.60%, Garlic 1.61%

6. Quality Rating - 4.26 by 5 average

  • Venkateswara Vegetables 4.29, Sri Sai Traders 4.27, Gudimalkapur Fresh Supply 4.26, Monda Market Agro Agencies 4.20
  • Highest dependency supplier does not have the highest rating

7. Category and Day Spend, 8. Payment Mode

  • Fruit Veg 49.23%, Root and Bulb 39.73% - together 88.96% of spend. Herb 5.80%, Leafy 5.24%
  • Weekend Sat and Sun 31.26% of weekly spend. Sunday highest at 15.80%
  • UPI 50.57%, Credit 7-day 39.67%, Cash 9.76% - credit is core to procurement

Key Insights

  • Two categories (Fruit Veg + Root and Bulb) drive 88.96% of spend - prioritize availability and pricing there
  • One supplier does 54.49% of business - dependency risk on price, quality and supply
  • Orders do not always go to cheapest: Sri Sai Traders was lowest-price option in only 47% of orders it won. Saving of Rs. 1,571 lost to relationship-based selection
  • All 11.2 kg rejected came from rating 2 by 5 purchases (only 7 of 112 lines) - rating 2 is a warning signal
  • Weekends need extra planning. Credit terms must be part of product
  • High variation items like Coriander Rs. 26.14 per kg need multi-supplier view

Proposed Solution - Digitalise, Onboard Local, Compare in One Screen

Digitalise by location, onboard local suppliers, compare price and quality in one screen to cut cost, cut dependency and cut rejection

  • Digitalise by location: location marketplace to discover serving suppliers and view prices before ordering. Solves phone and WhatsApp chasing. Backed by Rs. 1,571 saving and Coriander Rs. 26.14 variation
  • Onboard local suppliers: show serving suppliers instead of defaulting to one. Solves 54.49% dependency on Sri Sai Traders
  • Compare price and quality in one screen: price, quality rating, rejection history, availability, payment and delivery together. Solves rejection risk where all 11.2 kg came from 2-rated purchases

Product Workflow

  • Step 1: Select location - see serving suppliers
  • Step 2: Add vegetables - daily or recurring needs with quantities
  • Step 3: Compare suppliers - price per kg, quality rating, rejection history, availability, payment terms, delivery info
  • Step 4: Select supplier - on price and quality, not default habit
  • Step 5: Place bulk order - full order digitally
  • Step 6: Manage recurring purchases - save and reorder staples
  • Step 7: Track quality and performance - received qty, rejected qty, rating to improve future picks
